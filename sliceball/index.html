How it works

Slice. Fall. Unlock.

01

Slice

Stacks of cards hold the ball above the danger below. Slice through them at exactly the right moment.

02

Fall

Time it right and the ball falls clean to the next level. Mistime it, and the run is over.

03

Unlock

Levels get tighter as you go. Coins earned along the way unlock new balls to play with.
